Calendrop is a macOS app that reminds you of your Google Calendar meetings.

## Data Calendrop accesses

With your permission, Calendrop reads your Google calendar list and events,
read-only. It uses them only to show your upcoming meetings on your Mac.

## Storage and sharing

Calendrop runs entirely on your Mac and talks only to Google. Your sign-in is
kept in your Mac's Keychain, and your settings in your own iCloud Drive or on
your Mac. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, store or share
your data.

Calendrop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will
adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

Remove an account in Calendrop's settings, or revoke Calendrop's access at
[Google Account › Third-party apps & services](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
