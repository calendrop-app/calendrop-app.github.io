title: Getting started
description: Add your Google accounts, label them, and choose which calendars Calendrop reads.
---

## Add a Google account

1. Click the Calendrop icon in the menu bar and open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Accounts** and press **Add Account…**.
3. Your default browser opens Google's sign-in. Choose the account and allow
   Calendrop to see your calendar list and your events.
4. Return to Calendrop. The account appears in the list, and its meetings in
   the menu.

Repeat for each Google account. Nothing has to be set up beforehand.

Calendrop asks only to **see** your list of calendars and their events. If you
untick either on Google's consent screen, the sign-in is turned back with an
explanation; sign in again and leave both ticked.

While Calendrop waits for the browser, the accounts page shows **Cancel
Sign-In**. Use it if you closed the browser tab or Google showed an error page.
Starting another sign-in also replaces the one still waiting.

## Label and colour

Click an account to open its settings.

- **Label** is shown in the menu and on the cut-in instead of the address — for
  example `Work` or `Client A`.
- **Colour** marks the account's meetings in the menu and on the cut-in.

## Choose calendars

Under **Calendars**, each of the account's calendars has a switch. Until you
change one, Calendrop reads only the account's main calendar. Turn on any
others you want meetings from, such as a team calendar.

**Use Only the Main Calendar** goes back to reading just the main calendar.

## Remove an account

Open the account and press **Remove…**. Calendrop asks first, because removing
an account removes it from every Mac that shares your settings. Its sign-in
token is deleted from this Mac's Keychain. You can add it again later by
signing in.

## Several Macs

Your accounts and settings are kept in a file on iCloud Drive, so every Mac
signed in to the same iCloud account sees the same list. Sign-in tokens are not
shared: on another Mac, each account shows **Sign-in required on this Mac**.
Open it and press **Sign In…**; the browser opens with that account already
chosen. See [Sync](/docs/settings/#sync).
