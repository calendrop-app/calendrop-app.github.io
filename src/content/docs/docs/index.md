title: Introduction
description: What Calendrop is, who it is for, and what it does not do.
---

Calendrop is a macOS menu bar app that keeps your next Google Calendar meeting
in sight and, shortly before it starts, **cuts in**: it covers every screen
until you join the meeting or deliberately dismiss it.

## Who it is for

People whose day is split across several Google accounts — a company account,
a client's, a personal one — who sink into focused work and miss the start of
meetings because a notification banner is too easy to ignore.

## How it works

- **The menu bar** shows the next meeting and a countdown, such as
  `Weekly sync · in 12m`. The menu lists today's and tomorrow's meetings,
  coloured by account. Each one opens a submenu with its time, place, people
  and description, and actions to join its Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex link,
  copy the link, or open the event in Google Calendar.
- **The cut-in** appears a few minutes before a meeting on every display, above
  full-screen apps, the Dock and the menu bar. The largest thing on it is the
  button that joins the meeting. Ending it without joining takes a deliberate
  press and hold. See [The cut-in](/docs/cut-in/).
- **Every account, one timeline.** Meetings from all your Google accounts merge
  into one list, and an event you were invited to from more than one account
  appears once.
- **Follows your desk.** Plugging in or unplugging a display, or changing its
  resolution, rebuilds the cut-in to cover exactly the screens you have now.
- **Credentials stay on the Mac.** Your accounts and settings can follow you to
  your other Macs through iCloud Drive; the sign-in tokens never leave the
  Keychain of the Mac that signed in.

## What it does not do

- Edit, create or respond to calendar events. Calendrop only reads.
- Read calendars other than Google Calendar.
- Run on anything but macOS.
- Notify through Notification Center; the cut-in replaces that.

## Requirements

macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon, and a Google account with
Google Calendar.
