---
title: The cut-in
description: When the cut-in appears, what it shows, and the ways it ends.
---

The cut-in is Calendrop's reminder. Instead of a banner in the corner, it
covers every screen until you deal with the meeting.

## When it appears

- A set number of minutes before the meeting starts — two by default. You can
  change it, and zero means at the start. See [Settings](/docs/settings/#show-before-a-meeting).
- Still within a few minutes after the start, so a Mac that wakes from sleep
  just after a meeting began interrupts you too.
- Once per occurrence of a meeting. A meeting you joined or dismissed — from
  the cut-in or from the menu — does not cut in again, also after Calendrop is
  relaunched.

Some meetings never cut in: events you declined, all-day events and cancelled
events. Two kinds cut in depending on your settings:

- **Meetings without a video link**, such as in-person ones, cut in only if you
  turn that on.
- **Meetings you answered "maybe" to or have not answered** cut in unless you
  turn that off. They are marked as such on the cut-in and in the menu. An
  event you see through several accounts counts as accepted when any of them
  accepted it.

## What it covers

Every connected display, above full-screen apps, the Dock and the menu bar, on
every Space. When you connect or disconnect a display, or change its
resolution, the cut-in is rebuilt to cover exactly the screens there are now.

## What it shows

Each meeting on the cut-in shows its title, time and countdown, its account and
calendar, where it is, who else is invited and the start of its description.

The cut-in falls in from the top of the screen, like a blind coming down,
unless you turn that off. With **Reduce Motion** on in System Settings, it
fades in instead. Rebuilding it after a display change does not replay the
entrance.

## How it ends

Only by you. Escape and closing shortcuts such as ⌘W do nothing, and a cut-in
that has appeared stays even after the meeting is well under way.

- **Join** opens the meeting's video link in your default browser. It is the
  largest button on the cut-in. Google Meet links open as the account the
  meeting came from, even when your browser defaults to another Google account.
- **OK** takes the place of Join for a meeting without a video link. It only
  closes the cut-in.
- **Return** presses Join or OK when the cut-in shows a single meeting.
- **Hold to dismiss** is the small control in the corner. Press and hold it to
  settle every meeting on the cut-in without joining.

## Meetings close together

One cut-in shows every meeting that is due, and every meeting that starts
close to the first one or overlaps it, each with its own countdown and Join
button. A conflict is visible at once instead of as a series of interruptions.

- Joining one meeting also settles the meetings that overlap it: you have
  chosen.
- A later meeting that does not overlap the one you joined cuts in again before
  it starts.
- Meetings that become due while a cut-in is showing join it.

## Try it

To see the cut-in without waiting for a meeting, open **Settings ›
Appearance**, choose a sample under **Preview** — a single meeting, meetings
at the same time, meetings close together, a meeting without a video link, or
titles in many scripts — and press **Try Full Screen**. The trial uses sample
meetings, which never count as real ones. Close it the same way: Join, or hold
the corner control. A real meeting that comes due during a trial replaces it.
