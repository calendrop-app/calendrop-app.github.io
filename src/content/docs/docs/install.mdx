Calendrop runs on macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

## Download

<LatestDownload />

1. Unzip the download.
2. Move **Calendrop.app** into the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. Calendrop lives in the menu bar; it has no Dock icon.

## Allow the first launch

Calendrop is signed by its developer but not notarized by Apple, so the first
time you open it macOS refuses with a message that it cannot check the app for
malicious software. To allow it:

1. Close the message.
2. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and scroll down to
   **Security**. There is a line saying Calendrop was blocked.
3. Click **Open Anyway** and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
4. Click **Open** in the message that follows.

This is needed once per installed copy. After an update, macOS may ask once
more.

## Homebrew

```sh
brew install --cask calendrop-app/tap/calendrop
```

Update it with `brew upgrade --cask calendrop`. If macOS refuses to open it,
allow it as above.

## Updating

Quit Calendrop from its menu, replace **Calendrop.app** in **Applications** with
the new one, and open it again. Your accounts and settings stay. The first time
the new version reads your sign-in, macOS asks for Keychain access again; see
[Keychain asks again after each update](/docs/troubleshooting/#keychain-asks-again-after-each-update).

## Uninstall

1. Open **Settings › Accounts** and remove each account. This deletes its
   sign-in token from this Mac's Keychain. Removing an account removes it from
   every Mac that shares your settings; to leave the others alone, delete the
   tokens instead: open **Keychain Access** and delete the items named
   **Calendrop (your address)**.
2. Quit Calendrop and move **Calendrop.app** to the Trash.
3. To delete your settings too, delete the `Calendrop` folder in iCloud Drive,
   or `~/Library/Application Support/Calendrop/` if you do not use iCloud
   Drive. Its preferences are in
   `~/Library/Preferences/com.lambdalisue.calendrop.plist`.
4. To withdraw Calendrop's access on Google's side as well, remove it at
   [Google Account › Third-party apps & services](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
